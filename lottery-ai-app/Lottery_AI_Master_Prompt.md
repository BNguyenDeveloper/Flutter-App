# Lottery AI Master Prompt

## Core differentiator: Bao Nguyen Cross Region Pattern Engine

Logic: - Aggregate all Miền Nam results of day D - Find repeated /
dominant numbers (example: 30 appears 2+ times) - Check whether Miền Bắc
on day D also contains that number - Check historical Miền Trung offset
pattern (matching weekday / offset cycle) - If signals align, boost
probability for Miền Trung on day D+1

Example: - Miền Nam 02/05/2026 -\> number 30 repeats 2 times - Miền Bắc
02/05/2026 -\> has 30 - Historical Miền Trung offset date -\> has 30 =\>
Predict Miền Trung 03/05/2026 has elevated probability for 30
